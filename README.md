### 🏢 HRS ERP System
**ERP Suite** · A full ERP for a 100–300 employee company, built end to end as one Next.js application.

<a href="https://hrs-erp-system.vercel.app"><img width="100%" src="https://hxjvevbumbtifkxufzus.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/portfolio-media/Screenshot-2026-09-04-141305-1788509769084-7cil0m.webp" alt="HRS ERP System dashboard" /></a>

Eight modules on one Postgres schema: admin, HR & payroll, inventory, CRM, purchasing, sales, accounting and analytics. The accounting is **real double-entry**: invoices and payments post balanced journal entries, and the P&L and balance sheet are calculated from those entries, so they can't drift from the transactions. A custom **RBAC** layer controls access, and employee documents are served through a route that checks permissions on every request.

### 📱 HR Business Solution &nbsp;<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/now_building-2ea043?style=flat-square" alt="Now building" />
**Business Manager** · A mobile-first manager for small shops that sell through Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

<a href="https://hr-business-solution.vercel.app"><img width="100%" src="https://hxjvevbumbtifkxufzus.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/portfolio-media/dashboard-desktop-1790898185442-glej9n.webp" alt="HR Business Solution dashboard" /></a>

Orders, stock, customers, dues, invoices and profit reports in one **installable PWA**. Totals, dues and profit are **generated Postgres columns**, so reports always match the orders behind them. Saving an order is one atomic database call, triggers keep stock up to date, and team permissions are enforced by **row-level security**. Customers can get an A4 invoice or an 80 mm receipt over WhatsApp.

### 🧠 HR AI Whiteboard
**AI Whiteboard** · Turns a plain-English prompt into a real, editable diagram instead of a flat image.

<a href="https://hr-ai-whiteboard.vercel.app"><img width="100%" src="https://hxjvevbumbtifkxufzus.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/portfolio-media/Screenshot-2026-08-31-233605-1788198285513-q25nr5.png" alt="HR AI Whiteboard canvas" /></a>

An **Excalidraw** canvas with **Gemini** built into the sidebar. It reads your ideas and writes structured notes back onto the board, and a docs mode includes a paraphraser and AI humanizer. If one Gemini model hits its quota, a fallback chain switches to another so generation keeps working. Users sign in with Clerk, and boards autosave to Neon Postgres.

### 🛍️ Lagle Janaben
**Gift E-Commerce Platform** · *"Gifts that connect Hearts"*: a production store for a Bangladeshi gift shop.

<a href="https://tup5n9bf.insforge.site/"><img width="100%" src="https://hr-mern-portfolio.vercel.app/lagle-janaben.webp" alt="Lagle Janaben storefront" /></a>

A React storefront plus an **eight-tab admin dashboard** on a serverless Postgres backend (InsForge), so there's no app server to run. Every table has **row-level security**, and prices, promo codes and stock are recalculated on the server. Stock is decremented atomically when an order is placed, and **SSLCommerz** payments run through Deno edge functions that won't fulfil the same order twice. Cash on delivery is live.
